15Rrathë cyan

240 rrathë koncentrikë cyan që rrotullohen.

🐍 Kodi Python origjinalProject (EX) 15.py
import turtle

t= turtle.Turtle()

t.speed(100)
turtle.bgcolor("black")
for i in range(240):
    t.color("cyan")
    t.circle(i)
    t.left(5)
turtle.done
